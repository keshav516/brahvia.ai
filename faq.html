Brahvia AI: Frequently asked questions

Short answers about Brahvia AI, the AI website and web app builder.

What is Brahvia AI?

Brahvia AI is an AI workspace for building websites and small web apps. You describe what you want in plain words, Brahvia writes the HTML, CSS and JavaScript files, and you preview the result, change it, save versions, export the files and share a read-only link.

Is Brahvia the same as Sony BRAVIA?

No. Brahvia AI has nothing to do with Sony BRAVIA televisions. Brahvia is spelled with an h, and it is an independent AI website and web app builder.

Is Brahvia free?

New accounts get 20 free credits once, to try Brahvia. Free credits are not added again every day. To keep going you can use your own API key from Google Gemini, OpenAI or Claude (Settings, Use your own AI key): Brahvia then uses no credits and you pay your AI provider directly. Paid plans and credit packs are on the Pricing page. Paid plans also let you publish more sites and connect your own domain.

How many sites can I publish?

Each account can publish 5 new sites for free, for life (updating a site you already published is free). After that, a paid plan gives unlimited sites. Everyone, including paid users, can do up to 10 publishes in any 24 hours.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on paid plans. Publish your project to Netlify or Vercel, open Publish, and add your domain (for example www.yourname.com). Brahvia shows the DNS records to set where you bought the domain. Domains and DNS are handled by your host and registrar; it can take from minutes to a day to start working.

How do I pay? Is it safe?

Payments are handled by Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets). Brahvia never sees or stores your card or UPI details. Monthly plans are one-time 30-day passes and do not renew by themselves.

Is my API key safe?

Your key is stored encrypted on Brahvia's server, used only to answer your own requests, and never shown again (only its last 4 characters). You can remove it any time in Settings. API keys can act on your account with the AI provider, so only paste a key you are happy to use here, and set a spending limit with your provider.

Can I talk to Brahvia?

Yes. Tap the microphone button and speak in any language; Brahvia works out the language by itself and replies in the same one. Turn on the speaker button to hear replies. Reading aloud needs a voice for that language on your device, and speech-to-text accuracy can vary between languages.

How many credits does each action cost?

A chat message costs 1 credit, a code question 3, a website 8 and a web app 12. Changing a website or web app you already made costs the same as building it. If a request fails, the credits are returned. You always see the cost before you send.

What kind of sites can Brahvia build?

Static websites and small web apps made of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that run in the browser, such as landing pages, portfolios, menus, calculators and simple tools. Anything a site remembers is kept in the visitor's own browser. Sites built here do not have their own server, database, user login or payments.

Which AI does Brahvia use?

Brahvia sends your requests to Google's Gemini models from its own server, so your browser never holds Brahvia's AI key. If you add your own Gemini, OpenAI or Claude key in Settings, your requests go to that provider instead.

Can I download or move my website?

Yes. You can export the files of any project and put them on any static host, for example GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel.

Can I share a preview with someone?

Yes. Share creates a read-only link to one saved version. Anyone with the link can see it without an account. You can switch the link off at any time.

Can I publish my site from Brahvia?

Brahvia has a Publish option under Integrations that can send a project to your own GitHub account and turn on GitHub Pages. It is new, so check the result. You can always export the files and upload them yourself.

Does Brahvia remember my earlier chats?

Brahvia can keep a few short notes, such as your business name or preferred language, and use them in new chats. You can see, delete or switch this off in Settings. Passwords, keys and payment details are not saved.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Brahvia is designed to work on small phone screens as well as on laptops.

How is my data handled?

Read the Privacy Policy for the full details: what is stored, who processes it and how to delete it.
